# Contributing

Keep changes focused and describe the user-visible behavior they address. Include the connection type and OS versions when reporting a gesture or timing issue.

Run `./scripts/check-core.command` and the relevant build. Never replace a physical-device finding with a claim based only on simulator rendering or synthetic timing. Document what was measured and what still needs real-device validation.

Preserve English and Korean localization, control-release boundaries, fractional motion, pairing checks, and the minimal touch surface. Do not include QR keys, timing recordings from personal devices, signing profiles, archives, or credentials in commits.

Security-sensitive reports should go to dhleekr7@gmail.com instead of a public issue. Contributions remain subject to the repository's stated copyright terms and applicable third-party licenses.
